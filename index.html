How this was put together

The dates

Every song, air date and episode number was read straight out of the wikitext of the seven Wikipedia season articles, which record the Kellyoke for 1,232 of the 1,233 episodes. The totals reconcile exactly with the episode count each article declares. One morning, November 1, 2019, is listed as N/A, so it carries no entry here. She did sing that day: five shortened songs of her own, Miss Independent, Walk Away, Since U Been Gone, Stronger and Invincible, before announcing her Invincible Las Vegas residency. Not one of them was a cover, which is the likeliest reason the column is blank.

The videos

Matched by title and artist against the show's own YouTube channel and three fan archives that mirror the segment. Most clips state their own air date in the description, so 898 links are confirmed against the date shown here rather than inferred from the title.

What the labels mean

  • Sung by a guest is the show's Cameo-oke: 67 turns across 66 mornings when someone else took the opening number. It was rare until the end, one morning in season 4 and none before it, then 20 in season 6 and 46 in season 7. Hide them with the button above.
  • Her own song marks the 101 times she covered her own catalog.
  • with … means she sang it as a duet.
  • A note like Reba McEntire version means she sang that arrangement rather than the original.
  • date confirmed means the clip's own description states this air date.

Where it is thin

  • One cover has no findable copy: Day-O, October 31, 2024.
  • Only 13 of 67 guest turns were ever posted.
  • Where a song came back on a later morning and only one clip survives, every date points at that clip. That is 118 entries, each one identified by the clip dating itself to the other night.
